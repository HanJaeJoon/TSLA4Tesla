# English (en-US)

## App name (30 chars)

TSLA4Tesla - Stock Calculator

## Short description (80 chars)

How many Teslas can your TSLA shares buy? Find out with live stock prices.

## Full description (4,000 chars)

TSLA4Tesla tells you how many Tesla vehicles you could buy with your Tesla (TSLA) shares.

Features:
- Live TSLA stock price updates
- All models and trims: Model 3, Model Y, Model S, Model X, Cybertruck
- Enter your share count and instantly see how many cars you can afford
- Shows how many more shares you need to reach the next car
- Purchasing power history chart (1M / 6M / 1Y / 5Y)
- Approximate conversion to your local currency with live exchange rates
- Works offline with bundled market data
- Dark mode support

This app is for reference only. Displayed prices and exchange rates may differ from actual market prices.
